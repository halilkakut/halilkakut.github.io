About me

I am a Full Stack developer with 6+ years of software development experience, specialized in C#/.NET, ASP.NET Core, microservices and Angular. For 2 years I worked in the banking sector on BOA (Banking Operating Architecture), taking an active role in application development, maintenance and integration.

I have worked on enterprise call center, CRM and ERP applications, covering RESTful APIs, SQL, asynchronous messaging, caching, testing and CI/CD. I focus on .NET modernization, scalable services and sustainable solutions built on Clean Architecture, DDD and SOLID principles. I also integrate AI services into my own projects.
